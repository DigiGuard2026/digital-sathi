🚨 Scam Awareness

Learn to recognize common scams before they trick you.

🚨 Can You Spot the Scam?

OTP Scam

Never share OTPs with unknown people. Banks and real companies never ask for your OTP over a call, SMS, or email.

UPI Scam

A UPI PIN is generally used when sending money, not for receiving money. Be suspicious if anyone asks you to enter your PIN to "receive" a payment.

Fake Customer Care

Use official websites or apps to find customer-care information — not random numbers found through a search engine or social media.

Fake Job Scam

Be careful with job offers asking for money or sensitive information upfront. Genuine employers don't ask candidates to pay for a job.

Phishing

Do not blindly click suspicious links in emails or messages. Check the sender and the web address carefully before entering any details.

Fake Government Messages

Verify information through official government channels (like .gov.in websites) rather than trusting a forwarded message or SMS link.
