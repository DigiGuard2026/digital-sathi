🇮🇳 Government Digital Services

LEARN → PRACTICE → QUIZ → SCORE → PROGRESS

Simple Explanation

The government offers several official digital platforms to access documents and services safely — it's important to know how to identify the real ones.

Practical Examples

  • DigiLocker lets you store and access official documents like licenses and certificates digitally.
  • UMANG and MyGov are official apps for accessing various government services in one place.
  • Genuine government websites usually end in .gov.in or .nic.in.

Safety Tips

  • Always use the official government website or app.
  • Never click SMS links promising instant refunds.
  • Check that a website ends in .gov.in before entering personal details.
  • Never pay 'agents' extra money for guaranteed scheme approval.
  • Verify news about government schemes through official press releases.

Explore Each Topic in Detail

Tap on a topic to see simple steps and safety tips.

DigiLocker
  • DigiLocker is an official platform for storing and accessing digital documents.
  • You can access verified documents such as driving licences, certificates and marksheets.
  • Use the official DigiLocker website or app to sign in.
  • Keep your login details and OTP private.
  • Check that the document shown is yours before downloading or sharing it.
  • Avoid using DigiLocker through links sent by unknown people.
UMANG
  • UMANG brings many government services together in one app.
  • Use it to find and access services from participating government departments.
  • Download UMANG only from an official app store.
  • Check the service name and department before submitting information.
  • Never share your OTP, PIN or password with anyone.
  • Log out or lock your phone if you use UMANG on a shared device.
MyGov
  • MyGov is the Government of India's citizen engagement platform.
  • You can find government updates, campaigns, discussions and public participation activities.
  • Use the official MyGov website or app for information.
  • Check the date and source before believing a government-related post.
  • Do not share sensitive personal information in public comments or posts.
  • Follow links from MyGov only when they lead to a trusted official service.
Aadhaar-Related Online Services
  • Use the official UIDAI website for Aadhaar-related online services.
  • Services may include checking Aadhaar status, updating details and downloading Aadhaar-related documents.
  • Read the service instructions before entering your Aadhaar details.
  • Never share the OTP received for an Aadhaar service with anyone.
  • Avoid entering Aadhaar details on websites reached through random SMS or social-media links.
  • When sharing a copy, use the appropriate masking or privacy options when available.
Government Portals
  • Government portals provide official information and online services.
  • Many Indian government websites use domains such as .gov.in or .nic.in.
  • Type the official web address yourself when possible instead of using unknown links.
  • Check the website name carefully before logging in or submitting details.
  • Look for clear contact information and official department details.
  • Keep a bookmark of trusted government portals you use regularly.
Online Applications
  • Read the eligibility rules and instructions before starting an application.
  • Keep required documents and correct information ready before filling the form.
  • Enter your name, date of birth and other details exactly as required.
  • Review the form carefully before clicking Submit.
  • Save the application number or acknowledgement after submission.
  • Pay fees only through the official application portal and keep the receipt.
Digital Documents
  • Digital documents are electronic copies or verified documents that you can access online.
  • Store important documents in trusted services such as DigiLocker.
  • Use a screen lock and keep your phone or computer updated.
  • Share documents only with people or organisations that genuinely need them.
  • Check the recipient and document before sending it.
  • Keep a secure backup of important files when appropriate.
Checking Whether a Government Website Is Genuine
  • Check the full web address before entering any personal information.
  • For Indian government services, look for trusted official domains such as .gov.in or .nic.in.
  • Look for the correct department or organisation name on the website.
  • Be careful with small spelling changes in the website address.
  • A padlock or HTTPS helps protect the connection but does not by itself prove a site is official.
  • When unsure, reach the service from a known official government portal.
Avoiding Fake Government Websites
  • Do not trust websites that promise guaranteed approvals, jobs, refunds or benefits.
  • Avoid sites asking for unnecessary personal, banking or OTP information.
  • Do not download unknown apps or files from suspicious government-looking websites.
  • Do not pay extra money to an unknown person for guaranteed government benefits.
  • If a message contains a link, verify the service independently before opening it.
  • If a site looks suspicious, close it and use a verified official source instead.
Useful Official Resources
  • DigiLocker — use the official DigiLocker website or app for digital documents.
  • UMANG — use the official UMANG app or website for supported government services.
  • MyGov — use the official MyGov platform for citizen engagement and government updates.
  • UIDAI — use the official UIDAI website for Aadhaar-related services.
  • National Portal of India — use the official government portal to find information and links to government services.
  • For any service, start from a trusted official source instead of an unknown search result or message link.
