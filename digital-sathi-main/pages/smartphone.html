📱 Smartphone Basics

LEARN → PRACTICE → QUIZ → SCORE → PROGRESS

Simple Explanation

Your smartphone is a powerful tool once you know the basics — settings, connectivity, and how to keep it running smoothly and securely.

Practical Examples

  • Adjusting Wi-Fi and mobile data helps you stay connected and control how much data you use.
  • Installing and updating apps only from official stores like Google Play keeps your phone safe.
  • Managing storage and using screen lock protects your data and keeps your phone running well.

Safety Tips

  • Set a screen lock (PIN, pattern, or fingerprint).
  • Only install apps from Google Play Store or Apple App Store.
  • Keep your phone's software updated regularly.
  • Back up your photos and contacts to the cloud.
  • Turn off Bluetooth and Wi-Fi when not in use to save battery and reduce risk.

Setting Up a Phone
  • Turn on your phone and follow the on-screen setup steps.
  • Choose your language and connect to Wi-Fi to continue setup.
  • Sign in with a Google account (Android) or Apple ID (iPhone).
  • Set the correct date, time, and region.
  • Set up a screen lock (PIN, pattern, or fingerprint) right away.
  • Allow only the permissions the phone truly needs during setup.
  • Restore contacts and apps from an old backup if you have one.
Wi-Fi & Mobile Data
  • Wi-Fi uses home or office internet; mobile data uses your SIM's data plan.
  • Turn on Wi-Fi in Settings and tap your network name to connect.
  • Enter the Wi-Fi password carefully — it is case-sensitive.
  • Use mobile data when Wi-Fi isn't available, but keep an eye on usage.
  • Turn off Wi-Fi or mobile data when not needed to save battery.
  • Avoid using unknown public Wi-Fi for banking or important logins.
  • Check data usage in Settings so you don't run out before month-end.
Bluetooth
  • Bluetooth connects nearby devices like earphones, speakers, or smartwatches without wires.
  • Turn on Bluetooth from Settings or the quick-access panel.
  • Put the other device into "pairing mode" so your phone can find it.
  • Select the device name from the list and confirm pairing.
  • Turn Bluetooth off when not in use to save battery and stay safe.
  • Only pair with devices you know and trust.
  • Remove old or unknown paired devices from your Bluetooth list.
Installing & Uninstalling Apps
  • Open Google Play Store (Android) or App Store (iPhone) to find apps.
  • Search the app name and check the developer name before installing.
  • Tap "Install" and wait for the app to download.
  • To uninstall, press and hold the app icon and choose "Uninstall" or "Remove".
  • Only install apps from official app stores, never from random links.
  • Check app ratings and reviews before installing.
  • Remove apps you no longer use to save storage space.
App Permissions
  • Permissions let apps access things like camera, location, contacts, or microphone.
  • Apps ask for permission the first time they need it — you can allow or deny.
  • Only give permissions an app truly needs for its purpose.
  • Review and change permissions anytime in Settings > Apps > Permissions.
  • Turn off location or camera access for apps that don't need it.
  • Be cautious of apps asking for too many unrelated permissions.
  • Denying a permission is usually safe — the app can ask again if needed.
Updates
  • Updates fix bugs, improve speed, and add important security patches.
  • Turn on auto-update in Play Store/App Store settings for convenience.
  • To update manually, open the store and check "My apps" or "Updates".
  • Also update your phone's operating system regularly from Settings.
  • Update apps over Wi-Fi to avoid using mobile data.
  • Never download updates from links sent by SMS or email.
  • Restart your phone occasionally to complete pending updates.
Storage Management
  • Storage is the space where photos, apps, and files are saved on your phone.
  • Check storage usage in Settings > Storage to see what's taking up space.
  • Delete unused apps, duplicate photos, and old downloads regularly.
  • Move photos and videos to cloud storage or a memory card if needed.
  • Clear the cache of apps that take up too much space.
  • Keep some free storage space for smooth phone performance.
  • Back up important files before deleting anything you're unsure about.
Contacts & Emergency Contacts
  • The Contacts app stores names and phone numbers you can call or message.
  • Add a contact by tapping "+" and entering their name and number.
  • Save important numbers like family, doctor, and police clearly.
  • Set up emergency contacts (ICE — In Case of Emergency) in phone settings.
  • Some phones let you call emergency services quickly from the lock screen.
  • Back up your contacts so you don't lose them if the phone is lost.
  • Regularly remove old or duplicate contact entries.
Backup
  • Backup means saving a safe copy of your data, usually to the cloud.
  • Turn on auto-backup for photos, contacts, and apps in phone settings.
  • Backups protect your data if the phone is lost, stolen, or damaged.
  • Connect to Wi-Fi so backups don't use your mobile data.
  • Check backup status occasionally to make sure it's working properly.
  • Use a Google account (Android) or iCloud (iPhone) for backup.
  • Restore your backup easily when you set up a new phone.
Basic Troubleshooting
  • If the phone is slow, restart it — this fixes many small issues.
  • If an app crashes, close it and reopen it, or update the app.
  • If the phone won't connect to Wi-Fi, turn Wi-Fi off and on again.
  • If storage is full, delete unused files and apps to free up space.
  • If the screen freezes, press and hold the power button to restart.
  • If the battery drains fast, check which apps use the most battery.
  • If nothing works, note the problem and ask a trusted person or visit a service center.
